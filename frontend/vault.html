Keep MetaMask · Live on Sepolia

Protect what's in your wallet before Q-Day.

Your MetaMask address is guarded by one ECDSA key, and a large quantum computer can derive it. You don't have to switch wallets. Put ETH or tokens in the Q-Day vault: your ECDSA key can put money in, but only your post-quantum FORS+C key can take it out. Whoever breaks the ECDSA key finds nothing to move.

1Connect your wallet

MetaMask, or any wallet in your browser, on Sepolia.

2Your post-quantum guard key

A FORS+C key from 12 new words. It is its own secret: nothing about your ECDSA key or a MetaMask signature can rebuild it, so a cracked ECDSA key can't either. Write the words down: they are the only way to open the vault.

    4Take money out, post-quantum

    This page signs the withdrawal with your guard key: the owner, the token, the exact amount, where it goes and a deadline. Your wallet only submits it. Any wallet can submit it, so you can still withdraw if your ECDSA key is ever lost or broken.

    !Try it as a thief

    Pretend Q-Day came and someone cracked your ECDSA key. They can sign anything MetaMask can. Here they ask the vault for everything, with a FORS+C signature from a key of their own. The chain answers.
