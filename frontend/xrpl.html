    Private notes, by asset

    Your coins sit in a pool among notes of the same size. A payment proves it owns one of 8 of them without saying which, and the pool pays from its own account.

    The pool's keys sign once each, and every payout swaps in keys nobody has seen yet. That protects against a future quantum computer by timing: an attacker gets a few seconds per key. It is not a mathematical guarantee.

    Deposit

    A fresh tag on the pool's address. Pay it from any XRPL wallet or exchange: the sender sees the pool and the tag, never you. What arrives becomes notes only this browser can spend.

    Pay to
    Destination tag

    The tag is required: the pool refuses payments without one. Any amount works; what doesn't fill a whole note waits on the tag for the next payment.

    Testnet

    XRPL's testnet faucet pays this tag. The request goes through the relays like everything else; the ledger shows a fresh faucet account paying the pool.

    Buy with card or bank

    Opens MoonPay in a new tab. MoonPay knows who bought, how much, when, and this tag; the ledger shows MoonPay paying the pool.
