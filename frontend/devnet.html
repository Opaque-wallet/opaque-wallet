Your account's one key

Your account answers to one hash-based key, FORS+C, drawn from your recovery phrase. Nothing else signs: not its creation (its factory pays for that), not a transaction, not a bundler, because an EIP-8141 frame transaction has no envelope signature to need one.

Its 0x address is a contract's, fixed by its factory and your key's commitment: only a FORS+C signature moves what it holds. The key signs up to 8 transactions, counted by the account's nonce, and its last one hands the account to your next key.
