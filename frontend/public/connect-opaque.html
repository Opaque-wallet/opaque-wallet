A dapp, to try it on · Sepolia

Opaque, right beside MetaMask.

Any Ethereum dapp that lists wallets the standard way (EIP-6963, as wagmi, RainbowKit and Uniswap do) shows Opaque next to MetaMask. Pick it, and the account the dapp sees is a post-quantum one: every transaction it asks for is shown in Opaque's own window and signed with a FORS+C key. This page is such a dapp.

1Wallets in this browser

What this page found, as any dapp would: every wallet that answered its EIP-6963 request.

Opaque isn't in this browser yet. To add it:
  1. Download the Opaque extension (0.9 or newer) and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  3. In the extension, make a wallet, open Sepolia and give the account some test ETH (its faucet button). Then reload this page.
