5. Privacy and its limits

Deposits and settlements are public on the blockchain, note sizes are visible, and timing and patterns can still suggest links. Payments into one reusable address can be linked to each other. Opaque does not guarantee anonymity. Our Privacy Policy describes what each part of Opaque can see.

6. Your responsibilities

  • Keep your device and browser secure. Anything that can read your browser's storage can read your wallet.
