Hand the attacker the private key.

A quantum computer does one thing to Ethereum: from the public key a wallet published with its first transaction, Shor's algorithm recovers the private key. We can't run it yet, so this page plays its result. The attacker gets the victim's ECDSA private key and tries to take everything. FORS+C keys have no such shortcut: their secret sits behind keccak, which no quantum algorithm inverts.
